# Partners Services Agreement

**Arctic Fever | Christine Vroom Interiors**
Between AF ESTUDIO CREATIVO (Arctic Fever) and Christine Vroom Interiors, Inc.
Project: Salvaje, Partners retainer (Transformación)
Date: September 24, 2026

## 1. Parties

This Agreement is entered into by and between:

- **Service Provider:** AF ESTUDIO CREATIVO ("Arctic Fever"), based in San Pedro Garza García, Nuevo León, Mexico, represented by Esteban Villarreal, Founder.
- **Client:** Christine Vroom Interiors, Inc., based in California, USA, represented by Christine Vroom.

## 2. Scope of Work

Arctic Fever will provide ongoing design services for the Salvaje brand under the Partners Transformación package, with a capacity of up to 40 hours per month. Work may include:

- Collection books, including the requested change to the first mini book
- Catalogs by collection and by season
- Sample card or presentation piece for the sheet sets
- Brochures, sell sheets and editable templates
- Social media templates and launch content
- Press kit and press releases (copy and design)
- Design and content updates to the existing Salvaje website
- Other design work for the Salvaje brand, as agreed at the monthly meeting

Priorities are set at a monthly same page meeting. Hours are tracked and reported to the Client with each invoice. Unused hours do not carry over to the following month. Any request that would exceed the monthly cap will be flagged and quoted in writing before work begins.

Each piece includes 2 rounds of revisions. Once the Client signs off on a piece, it is considered final. Later changes to an approved piece count against the monthly hours or are quoted separately.

Files are delivered in PDF and editable formats (.AI, .INDD, .PSD or equivalent), including print-ready files where applicable, through a shared delivery folder.

## 3. Term

- Start date: October 1, 2026
- End date: March 31, 2027 (6 months)

The kickoff alignment meeting on September 24, 2026 is included at no charge. The Agreement may be extended month to month by written agreement of both parties.

If the Client delays feedback or inputs (renderings, product information, approvals), monthly fees remain due as scheduled and delivery dates move accordingly. If Arctic Fever delays delivery of an agreed piece, the Client's next payment will be postponed until delivery occurs.

## 4. Fees and Payment

- Monthly fee: $3,000 USD
- Term: 6 months
- Total: $18,000 USD

Arctic Fever invoices at the end of each month of service, starting October 31, 2026. Each invoice is due within 10 days of its date. Payment instructions are included on each invoice.

Fees do not include third-party costs such as printing, stock images, fonts, hosting, apps or shipping. These are approved by the Client in advance and either paid directly by the Client or billed at cost.

Late payments may result in a pause of work until the balance is settled.

## 5. Rights & Ownership

Arctic Fever retains full intellectual property rights over each deliverable until the invoice for the month in which it was delivered is paid in full. Upon payment, the Client is granted full usage rights for those deliverables, including commercial, print, digital and promotional use.

Concepts and drafts not selected by the Client remain the property of Arctic Fever.

Arctic Fever retains the right to showcase the work in its portfolio and social media after the public launch of Salvaje, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 6. Exclusions & Additional Requests

This Agreement does not include:

- Printing, sheet cutting or any physical production
- Photography, video production and tile renderings
- Paid media spend or ad management
- A new website build or platform migration
- Work for brands other than Salvaje

Additional requests outside this scope will be quoted separately as a new project, or billed at $75 USD per hour with the Client's written approval before work starts.

## 7. Legal Terms & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the United Mexican States (Mexico) and the State of California, USA.

In the event of any dispute, both parties agree to first attempt to resolve the issue through good faith mediation. If mediation fails, the parties agree to settle the matter through binding arbitration in Monterrey, Nuevo León, Mexico or Los Angeles, California, USA, as mutually agreed. Neither party may pursue litigation in public courts unless mutually agreed upon in writing.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days' written notice. The Client will pay the monthly fee for all months of service up to the end of the notice period, and Arctic Fever will deliver all files for work paid in full.

If the Client fails to make a scheduled payment, Arctic Fever reserves the right to pause work immediately until payment is received.

## 9. Other Agreements

This Agreement is between Arctic Fever and the Client only. Any agreement between the Client and littleMUCHO or John Ward is separate and does not change the terms of this Agreement.
